Privacy Policy – Sneaky Snatch

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Sneaky Snatch ("the game") is a single-player mobile game published by Saikaizen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or any account. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and fix problems (for example, which levels are too hard), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous gameplay events such as levels started and finished, coins earned and spent, ads watched and purchases made, together with device information (model, Android version, language, country), app instance and advertising identifiers, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is not linked to your name or any account, and is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms. Google keeps the detailed event data for 2 months; only aggregated reports are kept longer.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies — see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time from Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchasing "Remove Ads" turns off banner and interstitial ads.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Because your progress lives only on your device, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Data collected by Google for advertising and analytics is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Security

Data exchanged with ad, analytics and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
