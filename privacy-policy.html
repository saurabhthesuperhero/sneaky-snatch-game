Privacy Policy – Sneaky Snatch

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Sneaky Snatch ("the game") is a single-player mobile game published by Saikaizen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or any account. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising and the age question

The free version of the game shows ads served by Google AdMob. On first launch the game asks for your year of birth; the answer is stored only on your device and is used only to choose the right kind of ads:

  • Players under 13 (or if no year is given): ads are requested as child-directed and under the age of consent, limited to G-rated content and not personalised. No consent form is shown and the advertising identifier is not used for personalisation.
  • Players 13 and older: ads may be personalised. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies — see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time from Settings → Privacy options. Ad content is limited to PG.

You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchasing "Remove Ads" turns off banner and interstitial ads.

Children

The game can be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: for players who tell us they are under 13 the game requests only child-directed, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, which participates in Google Play's Families Ads Program, and the game has no accounts, chat or sharing. Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Because your progress lives only on your device, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Security

Data exchanged with ad and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
